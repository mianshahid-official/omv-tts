When the world falls to the infected, sound becomes your greatest enemy. Every snapped twig, squeaking hinge, or dropped tin can carries through the desolate streets like a beacon. The dead do not possess intelligence, but their auditory senses remain acutely attuned to human disturbance. If you are scavenging in dense urban ruins, ditch heavy boots for rubber-soled footwear. Wrap metal buckles in cloth to eliminate rattling. When clearing rooms, never speak above a whisper; communicate through hand signals instead. Remember, a single gun shot might save your life from one walker, but the thunderous echo will summon hundreds more from every dark alley within three miles. Move like a shadow, listen twice as much as you breathe, and let silence be your armor. In this dead world, noise is an invitation to your own funeral.
