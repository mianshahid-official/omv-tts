You can survive three weeks without food, but after three days without clean water, delirium will set in. In an apocalyptic collapse, municipal water pressure drops within forty-eight hours, and stagnant reservoirs quickly become contaminated by biological decay. Never consume standing surface water without vigorous decontamination. Fill every bathtub, sink, and bottle the moment utilities flicker. Construct a gravity-fed sand and charcoal filtration system using crushed gravel, sand, and activated charcoal from campfire embers. Even after passing water through multiple filtration stages, you must bring it to a rolling boil for a minimum of three full minutes to neutralize lethal pathogens. Store purified water in opaque containers away from sunlight to prevent toxic algae growth. Guard your clean water supply with your life. Dehydration will kill you long before the infected ever break through your barricade.
