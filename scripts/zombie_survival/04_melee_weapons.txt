Hollywood movies lied to you about firearms. In real life, ammunition is finite, firearms jam under dirty conditions, and the deafening muzzle blast draws every creature within a five-block radius straight to your position. For sustained daily survival, a durable close-quarters melee weapon is infinitely superior. Avoid lightweight baseball bats that splinter or decorative swords that bend on impact. Your primary weapon should be a solid steel crowbar, a reinforced trench hawk, or a forged demolition hammer. A crowbar serves a dual purpose: it effortlessly pries open locked supply doors and delivers crushing blunt force trauma capable of destroying the brain stem without getting stuck in bone. Maintain a secure grip with textured rubber tape and wear thick leather gauntlets to prevent biting scratches. Keep it silent, keep it sharp, and never rely on bullets you cannot replace.
