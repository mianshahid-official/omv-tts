Ground level is a death trap. When panic breaks out and the horde surges through city boulevards, roads turn into impassable corridors of shattered glass and stalled vehicles. Your top tactical priority during the first seventy-two hours is securing vertical clearance. Locate a multistory building with reinforced masonry, preferably concrete or steel. Clear the second floor immediately, then deliberately demolish or barricade the primary staircase with heavy debris. Use a retractable rope ladder or climbing webbing that can be hoisted out of reach. Ground floor windows must be boarded up completely, leaving no light leaks. From the third floor or rooftop, you gain vital three-hundred-and-sixty degree observation of migratory swarms, weather patterns, and smoke trails from other survivors. The high ground offers tactical visibility, natural defense against lumbering corpses, and time to formulate your next move.
